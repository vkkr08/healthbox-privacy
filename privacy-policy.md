# Privacy Policy for HealthBox

**Last updated:** September 2026

## Overview

HealthBox ("the app") is a personal health records application that helps you store and organize medical reports, test results, and checkup reminders for yourself and your family members. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Storage

All data you enter into HealthBox — including family member profiles, medical reports, blood test values, photos, and checkup dates — is stored **locally on your device only**. HealthBox does not use a server or cloud database, and your data is never automatically transmitted, uploaded, or shared with the app developer or any third party.

## Data You Control

- **Backup/Export:** You may choose to export your data as a backup file at any time. This file is created on your device, and it is entirely up to you where you save or send it (e.g., Google Drive, email, messaging apps). The app developer has no access to this file.
- **Import:** You may restore a previously exported backup file into the app at any time.
- **Deletion:** You may delete any member, report, or test entry at any time within the app. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data from your device.

## Permissions Used

HealthBox may request the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose:

- **Camera:** to photograph medical reports for storage in the app.
- **Photo Library / File Storage:** to select existing photos or files to attach as reports, and to save/restore backup files.
- **Notifications:** to send local reminders for upcoming checkups. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device.

No permission is used to collect or transmit data off your device.

## Third-Party Services

HealthBox does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. No usage data, personal data, or health data is sent to the developer or any external party.

## Children's Data

HealthBox may be used to store health records for family members, including children, at the discretion of the account holder (a parent or guardian). This data remains local to the device and is not transmitted anywhere.
